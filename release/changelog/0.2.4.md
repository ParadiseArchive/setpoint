# Setpoint 0.2.4

Made for modpacks: settings for pack makers, oil that works with other mods, and keys that never clash. For Minecraft 26.3 and 26.1.2. Back up your world before you update.

## New

- **Config for modpack makers and server admins:**
  - `power.*`: what each generator makes at full output, in FE/t (Stirling engine, biogas generator, steam turbine, diesel generator, fuel cell, wind turbine). JEI, the console and Jade show the numbers you set.
  - `showcase.allowCommand`: a server can switch `/setpoint showcase` off.
  - `worldgen.hookoniumOre`, in the new startup config: switch Hookonium ore off for packs that give it another way.
- **Oil is oil:** crude oil, diesel, kerosene and naphtha are in the common tags (`c:crude_oil`, `c:diesel`, `c:kerosene`, `c:naphtha`, the names PneumaticCraft uses too), LPG in `c:lpg` and the gases in `c:gaseous`. The distillation column takes other mods' crude oil, the diesel generator their diesel, and the burner burns their fuels; their machines take Setpoint's.

## Changed

- The vehicle keys (horn, lower the pallet, the forklift's forks) only count while you drive, so they no longer show a clash with vanilla sprint or other mods' keys in the controls screen.
