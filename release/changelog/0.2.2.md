# Setpoint 0.2.2

A power grid with batteries, operator gear, a mug of coffee and a box full of building blocks for a real plant. Back up your world before you update.

## New

- **Operator gear:** what an operator wears on the plant, as strong as diamond armour, made and repaired with Hookonium.
  - **Operator helmet:** a white hard hat with a brim all round. **Bump cap:** a yellow cap with a peak and a hard shell inside.
  - **Coverall top and trousers:** blue workwear with reflective bands. Dye it any colour in the crafting grid, like leather armour; the bands, the zip and the badge keep their colour.
  - **Safety boots:** black leather with steel toe caps.
- **Power grid:**
  - **Battery bank:** stores a million FE. On a power cable it takes what the machines leave over and gives it back when they need more than the generators make, so wind power keeps for a calm. A green bar on its front shows the charge; break it and the charge goes along.
  - **Power meter:** a box in a cable run with four lights that count up with the FE/t its cable carries. Also on the console, in Jade and on a comparator.
  - **Power page** on the video wall: what all generators make together, the batteries' charge, and a line per generator, battery and meter.
  - Machines on a cable now come first; batteries only get what is left, and give only to machines, never to each other.
  - The showcase plant has a battery bank and a power meter by its wind turbine.
- **Building blocks for a real plant:**
  - **Concrete plinth** for pumps and tanks to stand on, and a **bund wall** round a tank, joining up like any wall.
  - **Bollards**, an **epoxy floor** and an **epoxy walkway** with yellow lines; walkways side by side make one wide walkway.
  - A **pipe rack** portal: set them in a row and lay your pipes on top.
  - **Insulated pipe** and **insulated gas pipe**: real pipes in an aluminium jacket, for hot lines and steam.
  - A **wall sleeve** that takes a pipe straight through a wall.
  - A **cable tray** and a **transformer** that carry power like a cable.
  - A **local control station**: START and STOP push buttons that start and stop the machine beside it.
  - A **junction box**, **safety signs** (flammable, toxic, high voltage, hard hat area, ear protection, assembly point; right-click for the next) and an **MCC cabinet** for the electrical room.
- **Mug of coffee:** the coffee machine now pours a mug instead of giving the effect at once. Right-click and hold to drink it: a minute of speed and haste, and the mug is gone. With a coffee in your hand the machine pours you no other.
- **P&ID:** the wind turbine has its own symbol, and power cables are drawn as dotted purple lines beside the pipes (a cable under a pipe still shows). The showcase plant's P&ID shows its turbine and cable.
- **Power cable:** it now also pulls FE out of generators beside it, so other mods' generators that wait to be drained instead of pushing work with it too. Machines that only use power give none.

- **Handbook:** a chapter for the operator gear, and the new blocks in the chapters they belong to.

## Fixed

- **Handbook:** with eleven chapters the contents ran off the bottom of the book, and the last chapters could not be clicked. The contents now go on to a second page.
- Opening a menu could fail with Jade installed: the new Jade info for the cable and the wind turbine had no name in its settings.
- **Flare stack:** the guide beams no longer run through the caged ladder; on the ladder side they hold the ladder instead.
- **Pallet truck:** it no longer stutters behind you or in front of you. The game of whoever holds the tiller now moves it, the way a boat moves for whoever steers it.

## Changed

- **Wall clock:** a really round clock now, without see-through corners, with a grey bezel and a mark at every hour; a new inventory icon too.
- **Pipe wrench:** a new icon: a red pipe wrench with an open jaw and a brass nut.
