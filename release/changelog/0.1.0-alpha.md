# Setpoint 0.1.0-alpha

The first public alpha. Back up your world before you update to later versions: things will still change.

## What is in it

- **P&IDs:**
  - Draw any part of your plant as a P&ID.
  - Show it live in the DCS console or on a wall of P&ID boards.
  - Work the plant from the board: faceplates, pumps and hand valves.
- **Liquids:**
  - Pipes, tanks (stack them for a tall tank) and centrifugal pumps that cavitate.
  - Hand, check, drain and control valves, and a water intake.
  - Gravity flow between vessels, and every liquid with its own density and viscosity.
- **Containers:** drums and IBCs that keep their liquid when you carry them, and a floating roof tank for 8000 buckets with a roof that floats on the level.
- **Control:**
  - Level, flow and pressure transmitters.
  - DCS controllers that link themselves, with faceplates (AUTO, MAN and CAS, and PID tuning).
  - A DCS console with overview, trends and alarms.
- **Control room:**
  - Overview screens that make a video wall of up to 8 by 6 blocks: plant overview, alarms or trends.
  - An amber alarm beacon that turns and sounds until someone presses ACK.
  - An emergency stop that shuts the whole plant down at once.
  - Radios: the field operator calls in what needs a look.
  - Furniture: control desks, operator screens, a 24-hour chair you can sit in, a raised floor, ceiling lights, a wall clock, filing cabinets, a printer, a coffee machine, an exit sign, a blast door and wired glass.
- **Power:**
  - A Stirling engine and a biogas generator.
  - A coal-fired boiler with real steam pressure, trips and a safety valve.
  - A steam turbine, a diesel generator and a fuel cell.
- **Gases:**
  - Hydrogen, oxygen, nitrogen, steam, ammonia and LPG with real pressure.
  - Gas pipes, an electrolyser, a nitrogen generator and colour-coded gas cylinders.
  - A gas sphere for 3520 buckets of gas at 16 bar, and a compressor up to 200 bar.
  - Oxygen makes a boiler's coal last longer. Electrolyser, gas sphere and fuel cell together make a battery.
  - An ammonia reactor that wants 100 bar, and a mixer that turns ammonia into fertilizer.
- **Refinery:**
  - A pumpjack that pumps crude oil out of the ground anywhere.
  - A distillation column, eleven blocks high, that splits crude into LPG, naphtha, kerosene, diesel and residue.
  - Oil gives off vapour: blanket tanks with nitrogen, or use the floating roof tank.
- **Flare stack:** forty blocks high in a red and white derrick, the tallest thing in the mod. It burns what the plant cannot keep, from safety valves and from anything on its header above its set pressure. The flame grows with the gas, and LPG smokes black unless you blow steam into it.
- **Logistics:** pallets, an electric pallet truck, a forklift and pallet racking.
- **Factory blocks:** steel grating, grating stairs, handrails, caged ladders, steel I-beams, checker plate, hazard stripes, corrugated steel and industrial lamps.
- **Hookonium:** a purple endgame ore.
- **Help:**
  - The Setpoint Handbook and tooltips on every item.
  - An advancement tab.
  - Jade support, and every recipe shows in JEI.
