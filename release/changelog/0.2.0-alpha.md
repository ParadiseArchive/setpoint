# Setpoint 0.2.0-alpha

Gas lines you can control, a burner for what the refinery makes, JEI support and more for the control room. Back up your world before you update: things can still change between alphas.

## New

- **Gas valve:** shuts a gas line by hand. Shut, it splits the line in two.
- **Gas control valve (PCV):** lets gas down from a cylinder or sphere at high pressure into a line at the pressure you want.
  - Put a pressure transmitter against a gas pipe on the low side and a DCS controller against the transmitter. The controller finds the valve by itself and holds the pressure.
  - A little open lets little through, like a real valve trim.
- **Pressure transmitter on a gas line:** it now reads a gas pipe as well as a boiler or gas vessel.
- **Oil and gas burner:** set it under a boiler and it fires the boiler whenever no coal is burning.
  - It burns naphtha, kerosene, diesel, residue or crude oil from a pipe, or LPG or hydrogen from a gas line.
  - A pressure controller on the boiler turns it up and down, just like the coal fire.
- **Steam for other mods:** a pipe from another mod on top of a boiler takes its steam. Setpoint's steam is in the `c:steam` tag.
- **JEI:** every machine has its own tab showing what goes in and what comes out per tick, from the electrolyser to the distillation column.
- **Jade:** the burner shows its status and fuel.
- **Control room:**
  - The **printer** works: right-click it with paper and it prints the alarm log of the nearest console as a book.
  - **Console desk:** a DCS console built into a desk, with its three screens on top.
  - The overview screens have a fourth page, **P&ID**, showing the P&ID from the console live on the video wall.
- **P&IDs:**
  - The distillation column, the floating roof tank and the gas sphere are drawn across their whole footprint.
  - The emergency stop and the alarm beacon have their own symbols, which light up on the live drawing. The burner has a symbol too.

## Fixed

- A controller no longer jumps its output to 100 % when its measurement comes back, or when it links itself to a loop.
