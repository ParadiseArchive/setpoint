# Setpoint 0.2.1

Wind power, power cables and a whole example plant in one command. Back up your world before you update.

## New

- **Power cable:** carries FE from generators to the machines along it, so a machine no longer has to touch its generator.
  - It joins other cables and every block that gives or takes FE, from Setpoint or another mod, and turns corners by itself.
  - It holds nothing: power goes straight to the machines that want it, shared out evenly. Water flows round it.
  - Jade shows how many cables and machines a network has and how much FE it carries.
  - Recipe: three copper ingots between dried kelp, for eight cables.
- **Wind turbine:** a white tower thirty blocks high with three blades eleven blocks long, made after a real one.
  - Place it with one block, the foot, where the FE comes out; the rotor faces you. The circle the blades sweep must be clear.
  - Some 25 to 40 FE/t on a clear day, its full 100 FE/t in rain, a little more the higher it stands.
  - In a thunderstorm above 25 m/s it turns its blades out of the wind and stops (STORM STOP) until the gust is past.
  - The console shows it as WIND with the wind speed; Jade shows its FE on every block of it.
- **`/setpoint showcase`:** builds a whole working example plant next to you, for creative players and operators. Water from a pond, a boiler on a burner, a wind turbine, the pumpjack and distillation column, tanks for every product, an LPG sphere, the flare and a control room with its P&ID on the console and the wall.
  - It clears a space of about 34 by 34 blocks east and south of you, so use it in a superflat world. Type `/setpoint showcase confirm` to build it.

## Changed

- **Distillation column:** residue, diesel, kerosene and naphtha now also run out by themselves into a pipe that goes down to a lower tank. You only need a pump to lift them.
- **Flare stack:** a calmer, quieter roar without a beat you can hear, that carries less far.
