# Setpoint 0.2.1

Not released yet.

## New

- **`/setpoint showcase`:** builds a whole working example plant next to you, for creative players and operators. Water from a pond, a boiler on a burner, the pumpjack and distillation column, tanks for every product, an LPG sphere, the flare and a control room with its P&ID on the console and the wall.
  - It clears a space of about 34 by 34 blocks east and south of you, so use it in a superflat world. Type `/setpoint showcase confirm` to build it.

## Changed

- **Distillation column:** residue, diesel, kerosene and naphtha now also run out by themselves into a pipe that goes down to a lower tank. You only need a pump to lift them.
